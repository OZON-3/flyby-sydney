# Flyby Sydney

Fly a small jet over a pixel-art Sydney built from open map data and discover events as beacons of light
at their venues. Land anywhere to walk the streets, or bring your car to drive them.

Play it: https://ozon-3.github.io/flyby-sydney/

This repository holds only the built game. The events are invented samples at real venues.

**Test build.** The game now draws the city with its new renderer. Some things are not finished yet; the game lists
them the first time it opens. To play on the usual renderer, add `?renderer=cpu` to the address.

**New in this build:** fireworks over Darling Harbour; Luna Park's rides turning, with their band organ and screams
and the Hair Raiser timed to the real ride; the jet's new exhaust, hover glow and heat shimmer; things fade in and out
together with their shadows; the camera stays out of the jet when you take off from the car; and the Sources panel
links to the privacy page.

**Tell us how it felt.** Open the ? menu (or the known-issues panel) and tap "Copy report": it copies a short note
about your session for you to paste into a message. The game sends nothing by itself.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), through OpenFreeMap vector tiles (OpenMapTiles schema) and
the Overpass API; buildings also from Overture Maps Foundation and Microsoft's Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL);
elevation from the AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen terrarium). Credits are shown in the game.
